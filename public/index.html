How it works

Three steps, one safe ledger.

Talli keeps the interface simple because the work is specific: speak what happened, let the ledger resolve the customer and obligation, then review the recorded balance.

1

"Amaka took 25k goods today."

2

3

Your balance stays current

Balances, history, and amendments update safely. If the target is uncertain, Talli asks first.

Financial safety

Talli doesn’t guess with your ledger.

Financial state should not change when the target is uncertain. Talli separates interpretation from deterministic mutation, and if it cannot safely determine who or what an update refers to, it asks.

  • Deterministic ledger actions
  • Preserved amendment history
  • No mutation during clarification
  • Safe failure behavior
